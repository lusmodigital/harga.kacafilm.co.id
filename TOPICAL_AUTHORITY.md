# Topical Authority — harga.kacafilm.co.id

## Role and boundary

Harga.kacafilm.co.id is planned as a pricing-education layer for window-film buyers: it explains quote inputs, measurement units, scope, evidence, and comparison methods without publishing unsupported prices or duplicating root-domain transactional pages. Repository evidence does not prove a current independent deployment; `_redirects` presently sends the root and one legacy 3M location pattern to `kacafilm.co.id`. Any future publication must confirm canonical/deployment ownership first. Brand, specification, VLT/UV/IR/TSER, compatibility, installation, warranty, regional, vehicle, building, unit, and actual price claims require current written evidence.

## Context used

- No sitemap XML exists.
- `README.md` contains only the hostname.
- The 520-byte `index.html` is empty boilerplate and was skipped.
- `_redirects` defines two source patterns: `/` redirects to `https://kacafilm.co.id`, while `/2021/04/harga-kaca-film-3m-:kecamatan-:kota.html` redirects to `https://kacafilm.co.id/kaca-film-mobil-:kota`.
- Those redirects support a price-education concept but also gate whether this subdomain should publish independently or remain redirect-only.

## Ignored template noise

- The legacy `:kecamatan` and `:kota` placeholders are location swaps, not article ideas.
- The empty homepage, checked-file marker, and generic helper code provide no topical evidence.
- Root-domain service and city routes retain transactional intent; this plan does not recreate them as price or location landing pages.

## Internal-link rule

Every article links upward to its topic hub and toward the next pricing decision. Price fundamentals link to units and specifications; vehicle/building variables link to installation scope; scope pages link to quote comparison; comparisons link to warranty and total-cost review; any page mentioning a number links to the price-freshness method. Transactional links may point to a verified root-domain route only after canonical/deployment review and must not imply that this subdomain independently sells the service.
